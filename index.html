About Our Institution

A Foundation Built on Trust, Discipline, and Growth

At Hansfat International School, we believe that true education goes beyond the classroom. Located in Zenu, Ghana, we provide a disciplined, nurturing, and intellectually stimulating environment designed to unlock the unique potential in every child.

Our dedicated faculty works hand-in-hand with parents to ensure holistic development—balancing academic rigor with moral guidance, creativity, and leadership skills.
